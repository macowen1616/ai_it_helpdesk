# Printer Troubleshooting

## Printer Not Printing

If a printer is not printing, verify that the printer is powered on
and connected to the appropriate network or computer.

Check the printer queue for documents that may be preventing other
jobs from printing.

Verify that the correct printer is selected on the user's computer.

If the printer still does not print, restart the printer and try
printing again.

## Printer Not Responding

If the printer is not responding, check whether the printer is
connected to the network.

Verify that the printer is online and does not display an error
message.

Restart the printer and attempt to print again.

If other users are also unable to print, the problem may be related
to the printer or network rather than the user's computer.
