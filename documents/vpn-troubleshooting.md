# VPN Troubleshooting

## Unable to Connect to VPN

If a user cannot connect to the VPN, verify that the device has an
active internet connection.

Confirm that the user is entering the correct VPN credentials and
that the appropriate VPN client is installed.

Restart the VPN client and attempt to establish the connection again.

If the VPN still cannot connect, restart the computer and try again.

## VPN Disconnects Frequently

If the VPN connection repeatedly disconnects, check the stability of
the user's internet connection.

A weak or unstable internet connection can cause the VPN connection
to drop.

Restart the VPN client and reconnect to the VPN.

If the problem continues, determine whether other users are
experiencing similar connection problems.
